## Question and scope

Can a locally observed longitudinal acceleration mismatch, estimated from recent trusted wheels, predict the subsequent canonical double-wheel-outage error across source groups? It is not a physical brake gain and does not identify grade, load, resistance or actuator lag separately. Do not repeat H07/H36/H44 global parameter fits. Large/saturated canonical d is context, not proof of contamination. FD2 B0/B1 did not pass its group-robustness rule; no restriction to its few severe historical examples.

Runtime numerical code and canonical default remain untouched during foundation. Only after foundation PASS may the bounded propagation hook and one selected candidate be built. Diagnostic monitor is not an accuracy candidate. No GNSS/teacher/quality/bag/group/fault flags in runtime.

## One fixed local-state definition

Monitor the actual scalar v8 and held controller/front/rear inputs at their original arrival order and 20 Hz output grid. Maintain at most 16 model-increment endpoints and 16 unique trusted wheel-pair records, never growing histories. Compute the same bounded pre-assimilation prediction acceleration as v8, including actuator update, acceleration/speed clips and numerical zero-crossing guard. Integrate this acceleration with its actual dt; linearly interpolate the past integral at a pair's mean source time, only between already available endpoints. Model integral is a diagnostic coordinate, not extra position or feedback. No extrapolation beyond endpoints and no future samples.

A trusted update requires mode FUSED, both statuses ACCEPTED, valid controller with u < -command_deadband, both wheels finite/fresh and source skew within canonical bound, abs(front-rear)<=0.2 m/s, abs(mean wheels)>0.5 m/s, and strictly new pair time. Fit an ordinary least-squares slope of z - A_model against centered pair source time over the last 1.0 s, at most 16 records. Require >=6 pairs and span >=0.6 s; front and rear slopes each <=3 m/s^2 in magnitude and their difference <=0.25 m/s^2. Residual is clipped to +/-0.3 m/s^2. Record unclipped and clipped values, slopes, support, mode age, d, drive_a and command. Bad raw/model/zero-lock/quarantine evidence clears support/state. Fresh duplicate ticks do not count as new support. Missing/stale samples retain the last supported residual without updating; a source gap >max_age between new pairs restarts support. Leaving braking, initialization, stopped, reset, invalid command clear state.

ONE forgetting mechanism: exponential decay from last trusted residual update with tau=2.0 s. No tau or history-window sweep. Future runtime correction only while both supplied wheel samples are invalid by original max_age_s and controller remains valid braking, abs(inner speed)>.5, and last support was qualified. Fresh but rejected/common-mode/locked wheel values do not qualify as missing. Correction bound 0.3 m/s^2 times a single gain in [0,1]. No modification of F/P/B, d estimator, Q/R, wheel/readout gains, timing, stops, recovery or covariance. Any propagation hook is identity in canonical v8; during healthy updates correction is identically zero. Accumulated signed s is never erased.

## Foundation gates fixed now

Coverage: >=30 eligible distinct braking anchors overall; >=3 source groups each with >=2 eligible anchors; all available source groups retained. Vehicle-specific generalization is claimed only with >=3 eligible groups per vehicle; otherwise explicitly underpowered, not silently discarded.

Transfer: out-of-group weighted normalized MSE of beta*x vs y must be >=5% better than the zero-correction prediction; >=2/3 represented groups must have strictly lower MSE; deleting any one represented group from the already out-of-fold results must preserve positive aggregate gain. At h=2, among abs(r_pre)>=.05 and abs(y_2/2)>=.05, require >=10 anchors in >=3 groups and weighted sign agreement >=.60. The no-intercept unconstrained fit coefficient must be positive for every training fold (otherwise no consistently transferable sign). All gates are conjunctive. These are prospective practical criteria, not p-values or a claim that correlated ticks are independent. No thresholds are modified after results.

Failure of persistence/transfer with sufficient coverage => FOUNDATION_FAILED, no runtime candidate, no check/development/validation. Inadequate target coverage => INCONCLUSIVE at foundation. Report terminal and fault signed-error correlations even on failure; do not remove hard cases. An absent prediction is not a zero RMSE.
